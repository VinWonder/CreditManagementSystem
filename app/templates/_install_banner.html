Install Duka Credit

Add it to your home screen and open it like an app: faster, full screen, one tap away.

  1. Tap Share in Safari's toolbar.
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen.
  1. Tap menu at the top right of Chrome.
  2. Choose Install app. Avoid "Add to Home screen": that only makes a shortcut with Chrome's logo.

This icon is a Chrome shortcut, so the Chrome logo shows first when it opens. To get the full app:

  1. Press and hold this Duka Credit icon on your home screen and remove it.
  2. Open {{ request.host }} in Chrome, tap and choose Install app.
